\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We investigated whether psychological signals in developers' communication, \ie emotions, belonging, and received recognition, anticipate disengagement from open-source projects. To this aim, we built a mining pipeline that operationalizes these signals together with structural and workload measures, and we fine-tuned an emotion classifier that improves over existing tools on GitHub. \tbd{[Placeholder: one or two sentences with the main results of RQ\textsubscript{1}--RQ\textsubscript{3}.]} Our results suggest that turnover research should look not only at \textit{what} developers do before leaving, but also at \textit{how} they feel and relate to their community, and that leaving a project does not necessarily mean leaving open source. As future work, we plan to validate the signals against developers' self-reports and to study how the arrival of AI-generated contributions changes them. All data and scripts are available in our replication package~\cite{replication}.
